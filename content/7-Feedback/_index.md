### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment in the program is highly professional, open, and dynamic. The workspace is logically arranged and fully equipped, creating a comfortable atmosphere that helped me maintain high focus at work. Everyone in the team is always open, friendly, and willing to support and answer questions, even outside of official working hours.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentor was extremely dedicated and used great pedagogical methods. The mentor always encouraged me to actively research and propose solutions independently before providing comments or optimal guidance, which effectively fostered my independent thinking. The admin team worked very professionally, quickly supporting administrative procedures and documentation, and providing all necessary work tools to create the best conditions for interns.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The assigned tasks closely aligned with and effectively utilized the foundational knowledge I accumulated at university. At the same time, the work exposed me to many practical new technological areas (especially Cloud and AI) that I hadn't had the chance to dive deep into before. This helped me reinforce theoretical knowledge while building confidence when applying it to real-world projects.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
The internship provided me with comprehensive growth opportunities. I not only improved my technical skills (programming, system analysis, using project management tools) but also honed essential soft skills such as teamwork, time management, and professional workplace communication. Practical experience shared by my mentor also helped me clarify my future career orientation.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The workplace culture is very positive, respecting differences and encouraging innovation. Team spirit was clearly demonstrated through seamless coordination among members; when facing difficult problems or tight project deadlines, everyone was always ready to share workload and support each other enthusiastically regardless of position or experience. This made me feel truly connected and an integral part of the team.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The company maintains a transparent and punctual internship allowance policy, along with flexible time management that allows students to balance university studies and work responsibilities. Furthermore, having the opportunity to participate in internal training and technical knowledge-sharing sessions was a major plus.

### Additional Questions
- **What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**  
  What satisfied me the most was my significant personal growth in both working mindset and practical technical skills. I feel fortunate to have had the opportunity to "learn by doing" in an open environment, with close guidance from my mentor and mutual respect from colleagues.
- **What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**  
  The company could build a more standardized and centralized onboarding roadmap during the first 1–2 weeks so that future interns can grasp the overall system and workflows even faster.
- **If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?**  
  Definitely yes. This is an ideal environment for students who want to experience a real working environment, gain hands-on experience with advanced technologies, and accelerate their personal growth under the enthusiastic guidance of experienced seniors.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations
- **Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**  
  Increase the frequency of short, specialized sharing sessions (Tech Talks) between different intern groups to allow cross-team networking and learning about different project areas within the program.  
- **Would you like to continue this program in the future?**  
  I strongly desire to have the opportunity to stay connected, collaborate long-term, or become a full-time employee at the company/First Cloud AI Journey program after graduation.
- **Any other comments (free sharing):**  
  I would like to express my sincere gratitude to the First Cloud AI Journey Organizing Committee, my mentor, and the admin team for creating favorable conditions and constantly supporting and accompanying me throughout this internship.
